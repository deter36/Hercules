# Product principles

- Portrait mobile first for future presentation work.
- UI renders engine state and submits player choices; it owns no rules logic.
- Preserve recognizable tabletop identity and physical component conventions.
- The player owns tactical decisions. Validation may use explicitly declared
  recorded human choices or constructed scripted choices, without optimization.
- Show important state: resources, phase, Labor health/track identity, die identity
  and availability, pending decisions and unresolved/provisional rules.
- Deterministic auditability and correct state precede visual polish.

These are durable future constraints. This experiment does not include UI work.
